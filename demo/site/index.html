Café Olivine: order from the table, run the floor from a phone

The customer scans the table's QR code and orders; waiters confirm on their phones; the bar and kitchen see what to prepare. In production a Windows agent then types each order into the café's existing till. Interfaces are in French (staff) and French / English (menu).

  1. Order from table 7 on the left phone.
  2. On the right, the waiter Sara gets it in Commandes: confirm.
  3. Log out, sign in as barista or cuisinier (password demo) and tap Prêt.
